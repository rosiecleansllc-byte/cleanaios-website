Questions

Asked by owners
just like you.

How much does it cost?
Three ways in, depending on where you are. The DIY Toolkit — the full Rosie System in a box — is $297 one time. Live work starts with the Foundations Track: three 90-minute sessions, $1,495 at the founding rate ($1,997 public). The standalone programs — You + AI, Financial Clarity & Pricing Confidence, and Competitive Positioning — are $997 founding / $1,500 public each. The Core Program, the full seven-module build, is scoped on a call so the price matches the business. No subscriptions. No surprise upsells inside a program.
How long does it take?
Foundations is three 90-minute sessions, scheduled at your pace. Standalone programs run four to five sessions. But the honest answer is that you don't wait until the end to get value: every session finishes with a documented, working asset running in your business before the next one starts. Session one already changes how your week runs.
I'm not tech-savvy. Will this work for me?
It was built for owners, not technicians. Phase 1 starts with prompt cards and the tools already on your phone — no new software, nothing to install. Anything technical gets built live in session, with you watching, and documented so you can run it yourself afterward. If you can run a cleaning schedule, you can run this system.
What if my team won't use it?
That's the most common fear owners bring in, and the build is designed around it. Your voice and operating rules go onto a personal prompt card your team uses directly. SOPs are written for the person doing the job. You confirm the operator model — who owns what when you're not there. And the daily rhythm is 15 minutes, because a system your team can't hold is a system that doesn't exist.
What do I keep if I leave the program?
Everything. Every document, SOP, script, prompt card, and plugin built in your sessions is yours, stored in your own workspace and deliverables vault. The Margin Stabilization Plugin is live in session, then yours to keep. There's no subscription holding your business hostage — what we build in your business stays in your business.
Do I need specific software?
No. The system is built around the booking and client tools you already use, plus an AI assistant. Owners in the program run different booking platforms — the system doesn't care which one. Phase 1 requires no new tools at all.
